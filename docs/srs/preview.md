# Preview

What an author can do with a form in the designer's Preview before anyone uses it: fill it in as a respondent would, check it, step through it, see it at simulated sizes, and read the answers it collects. Area code `PREV`.

The control that opens Preview, and the panes Preview hides and restores, belong to `WORK`. How Preview reaches assistive technology, including its live notes and the keyboard scrolling of its views, belongs to `A11Y`. What a rendered form, a condition, a step or a rule does in general belongs to `FILL`, `FLOW` and `VAL`; these rows cover what Preview offers on top. The canvas while editing, including the "Hidden in preview" label, belongs to `BUILD`.
